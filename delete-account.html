Delete Your Account

To delete your iNeed account and all associated data, follow these steps in the app:

  1. Open the iNeed app.
  2. Tap the menu icon on the left side of the screen.
  3. Tap "Delete Account".
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Once confirmed, your account and all data associated with it (including your posted items and profile information) will be permanently deleted from our systems.

Note: If you are unable to access the app but still wish to request deletion of your data, please contact us at ybg1998@gmail.com with your device ID or account details, and we will process the deletion manually.
